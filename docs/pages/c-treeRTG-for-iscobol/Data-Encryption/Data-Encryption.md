# Data Encryption

## Overview

Data encryption serves as a cornerstone of modern cybersecurity, offering robust protection for sensitive information alongside distinct operational trade-offs. On the positive side, it safeguards confidential data from unauthorized access, whether the information is traveling across networks or resting on a server, while also ensuring organizations comply with strict regulatory standards. However, these security benefits come with costs. The continuous encryption and decryption processes introduce a notable performance overhead, consuming valuable CPU resources that can slow down system speeds. Additionally, managing encryption keys introduces high stakes, as losing a key means permanent data loss and requires rigorous backup procedures.
