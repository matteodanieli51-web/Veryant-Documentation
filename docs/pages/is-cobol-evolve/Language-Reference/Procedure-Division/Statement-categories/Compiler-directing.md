### Compiler-directing

A compiler-directing statement is a statement that causes the compiler to take a specific action during compilation. isCOBOL supports the following compiler-directing statements: [COPY](../../Compiler-Directing-Statements/COPY), [EJECT](../../Compiler-Directing-Statements/EJECT), [SKIP](../../Compiler-Directing-Statements/SKIP), [REPLACE](../../Compiler-Directing-Statements/REPLACE) and [SKIP3 skips three lines before the next line](../../Compiler-Directing-Statements/SKIP).
