See [Importing programs from AcuBench](../../is-cobol-IDE/Importing-programs-from-AcuBench/Importing-programs-from-AcuBench) and [Importing programs from Totem](../../is-cobol-IDE/Importing-programs-from-Totem/Importing-programs-from-Totem) for more information.
