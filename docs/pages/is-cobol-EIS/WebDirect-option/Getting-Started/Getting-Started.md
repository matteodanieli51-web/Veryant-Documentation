## Getting Started

The setup of a WebDirect environment requires the following steps:

1. [Download and install isCOBOL EIS](./Download-and-install-isCOBOL-EIS)
2. [Download and install isCOBOL WebDirect](./Download-and-install-isCOBOL-WebDirect)

In order to activate your isCOBOL Evolve products, you will need the e-mail you received from Veryant containing your license key. Contact your Veryant representative for details.
