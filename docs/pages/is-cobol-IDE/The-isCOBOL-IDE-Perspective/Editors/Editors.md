Editors create and modify resources. By default, editors are placed in the center of the IDE window. The following editors are available in the isCOBOL IDE:

- [Code Editor](./Code-Editor)
- [Copy View Editor](./Copy-View-Editor)
- [Screen Designer](./Screen-Designer)
- [File Designer](./File-Designer)
- [File Section Designer](./File-Section-Designer)
- [Working Storage Designer](./Working-Storage-Designer)
- [Local Storage Designer](./Local-Storage-Designer)
- [Thread-Local Storage Designer](./Thread-Local-Storage-Designer)
- [Linkage Section Designer](./Linkage-Section-Designer)
- [isCOBOL Service Editor](./isCOBOL-Service-Editor)
- [JOE Editor](./JOE-Editor)
